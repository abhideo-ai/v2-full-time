The permanent residual — the part no wording can fix

He has no measured production outcomes for any of the Amura services, and he never will. He left VoltusWave before those numbers were ever captured. This is a fact about the record, not a weakness in the work.

Every latency and quality figure in the corpus is a pre-registered acceptance target, and the corpus says so itself in its own words. The warm-path 99th-percentile latency under 200 milliseconds, the normalised discounted cumulative gain at rank 10 (NDCG@10) of 0.75 or better — those are targets that were agreed before the work, not results measured after it. CLAUDE.md is explicit that stating a target as an achieved result breaks the honesty rules, so they must never be written as outcomes.

The same discipline applies to the single worst honesty landmine in the corpus. KQ2’s served-cell table prints numbers that look exactly like a measured result — 412 and 217 patients, 0.58 and 0.34, a 24-percentage-point difference, a maximum standardised mean difference of 0.08 — and the page labels them, in its own words, an illustrative response shape, not a production measurement. None of those figures may ever appear on a résumé or be spoken in a room as an outcome. The same goes for KQ1’s “300 patients in India on protocol A”.

So the Amura bullets carry design and scale markers instead: 80 or more chronic conditions, 9 to 10 years of patient history behind the platform, a 6-hour cache time-to-live on KQ4, a 90-day outcome-maturity window, twenty adversarial-review findings absorbed into a hardened version two, 13 of 13 local findings closed. Those are all facts from his own documents, and the 9–10 years of history is the one that makes the rest credible — as-of featurisation and leakage-safe labelling are only meaningful with years of longitudinal data behind them.

The practical consequence for this seat. Tachyon asks for production work “not a POC”. He can answer that honestly and completely: the system shipped, it is deployed, here is its architecture and here is what was designed into it. What he cannot do is follow that with a measured production number, because there is not one. That is the residual, it is permanent, and it is why the AI criteria sit at 9 rather than 10.
