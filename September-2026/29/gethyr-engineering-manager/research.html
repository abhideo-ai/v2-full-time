5. What an Engineering Manager or Senior Engineering Manager seat usually is (general context)

General context, not facts about this seat. No reliable India-specific source was found; the sources below are general, and the India-startup lines are our inference from them.

  • People, delivery and hiring. GitLab’s public job description says an Engineering Manager will “Manage a team of engineers”, “Actively seek and hire” talent, and “own the delivery of product commitments”, while giving “guidance and coaching to team members on technical contributions, product architecture”. Independent, one company’s definition (GitLab handbook).
  • How hands-on. Gergely Orosz (The Pragmatic Engineer) treats staying hands-on as a deliberate effort for managers who are “no longer coding on a regular basis” (Pragmatic Engineer, 14 Feb 2023). LeadDev’s Engineering Leadership Report 2026 found “37% of engineering leaders are doing more hands-on technical work than in 2025”, and the share of managers coding directly rose “from 20% in 2025 to 35% in 2026”, partly because AI tools lowered the barrier (LeadDev, 10 Aug 2026). Independent, survey-based.
  • Engineering Manager against Senior Engineering Manager. At most companies a Senior Engineering Manager has a larger scope: more engineers, often through other managers, or several teams. The GitLab page does not spell out the difference, and this post offers both titles, so the level will be decided by the client Inference.
  • At a startup. “Hands-on technical leader with a strong startup mindset” usually means the manager still designs and reviews, sometimes codes, and carries hiring and delivery for a small team Inference from the sources above. GetHyr’s own July post for a similar seat spelled it out as “Own hiring, mentoring, and performance management” while staying “hands-on” (section 3).
  • Why it matters here. This workspace’s rule is individual-contributor seats first. A manager seat can still be sent; the questions that decide how far it is from an individual-contributor seat are the number of direct reports and the share of hands-on work (forcing questions).
6. Sending a résumé to a recruiter by email: what to check first (general practice)

Neutral practice, not advice about GetHyr in particular.

  1. Know the client before you are submitted. A recruiter who will not say which company it is hiring for gives the candidate no way to judge the seat or to avoid a double submission. Harrison Barnes of BCG Search writes that if a recruiter submits a résumé “without your express prior consent, they certainly have behaved in an unethical way” Independent (BCG Search, a legal-recruiting firm writing about its own trade).
  2. Consent for each submission. Ask the recruiter to confirm in writing that the résumé goes only to the named client, only for this role, and not to anyone else without asking again. In India the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 sets the bar for consent: it “shall be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous with a clear affirmative action” and limited to the stated purpose Register, the Act itself, section 6(1) (MeitY, the Act). How the Act applies to recruiters in practice is not settled here.
  3. Exclusivity and duplicates. If two recruiters, or a recruiter and the candidate himself, send the same résumé to the same company, the company may see a fee dispute and a disorganised candidate. Some recruiters ask for a “right to represent”: written permission to represent the candidate for one role. Lutz Talent advises checking that such an agreement covers one position only, which employers it covers, how long it lasts, and whether it stops you working with other agencies Independent (Lutz Talent, 24 Feb 2020).
  4. Contingent or retained. In a contingent search the agency is paid only when its candidate is hired, and the client may use several agencies at once; in a retained search one firm works exclusively, usually for director-level seats and above Independent (TalentRise). Which one this is tells him whether other agencies may already have sent candidates to the same client.
