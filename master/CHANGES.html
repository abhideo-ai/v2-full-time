3.1 · A factual correction — not an improvement

Why it is wrong: KQ1's own stack table names Elasticsearch as the “Authoritative serving index — dense_vector filtered kNN”, and says the graph database “serves audit and drill-down only, so serving latency never depends on its tail.” The vectors are in Elasticsearch; Neptune is deliberately off the serving path. Why it matters more than a tidy-up: the truth is the better bullet. “RAG over a graph” is a claim anyone can make. “Denormalised precedent cards into the search index so serving latency never depends on the graph's tail” is a decision with a reason behind it — and Elasticsearch is already on your safe-to-claim list.

Proposed for quick-skills-ee. Skills lines carry no number requirement — that rule applies to experience bullets — so none of this depends on metrics you cannot supply.

  • Causal inference on observational data — propensity matching, inverse probability weighting, standardised-mean-difference balance gates, caliper stability, seeded bootstrap intervals

For a Principal artificial intelligence or machine-learning seat the technique list is the real differentiator: plenty of candidates list LangChain, almost none can say they built a gate that makes an unbalanced comparison unrepresentable in the output type.
