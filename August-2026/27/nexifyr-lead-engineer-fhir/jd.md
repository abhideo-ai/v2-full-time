## About us

An AI-native clinical platform for ADHD and autism assessment, and the foundation of a mental
health operating system. Founded and clinically led by practising NHS psychiatrists, backed by a
£1M seed round, building for real clinical use rather than wellness-adjacent software.

**Horizon one: fix assessment.** UK adult ADHD and autism services have waiting lists measured in
years, and clinicians spend a large share of every assessment on structured documentation that AI
can now do properly. Runs the full pathway: digital screening and rating scales, collateral
information from parents and teachers, and an ambient-AI clinical assessment that produces a
completed DIVA-5 and draft psychiatric report for clinician review. The clinician stays in the loop
and signs everything. "We are compressing the admin, not the judgement."

**Horizon two: the mental health OS.** Assessment is the wedge, not the destination. Every
assessment generates structured longitudinal data that today gets flattened into unsearchable free
text. Building toward a clinical operating system where a patient's history is properly structured
and instantly legible to any clinician who picks up their care — spanning diagnosis, treatment,
risk and follow-up rather than a single episode.

You'd be the **second engineering hire** and the person who owns how this gets built. Early on that
means writing most of the code yourself. Within 12 months it means hiring and leading a small team,
and owning technical assurance for the NHS: ISO 27001, DSPT, DTAC, and the DCB0129 clinical risk
process alongside their Clinical Safety Officer.

Ground-floor role with founding-level equity. **The CTO title is on the table on a defined milestone.**
