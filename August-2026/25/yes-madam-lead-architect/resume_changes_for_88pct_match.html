Ramp item, capability cap, or neither

The distinction that matters most on this page. A ramp item is a named tool he has not used where he plainly has the underlying capability — the rulebook says score that generously. A capability cap is a genuinely core required technology that is absent, and it caps the score honestly. This rubric records no capability cap on any of its seven criteria. But "no capability cap" is not "a rewrite closes it", and the two must not be confused. Three of the four groups below cannot be moved by editing any file.
