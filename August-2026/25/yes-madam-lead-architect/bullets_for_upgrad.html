Read this before pasting anything

The exporter is the path, not this page. upgrad_apply.py overwrites all ten parsed sections from this workspace's upgrad_resume.html on every run, dispatching a synthetic clipboard event straight into the editor — which is the only route that carries both bold and bullets. If the export has run, none of these lines needs pasting by hand. This page is for reading what the bot will write, and for one-off single-line fixes.

Why the many small buttons. No markup shape gives both bold and bullets through a real clipboard paste. A <ul><li> keeps the bullets and loses the bold; several paragraphs at once keep the bold and collapse into one line; one paragraph pasted alone is the only shape that keeps both a bullet and its bold. So the per-line buttons are the mechanism, not clutter. Every button carries data-copy-html="1"; nothing on this page uses a styled <span>, because upGrad strips those too.

A copy block is one whole section. Each block below carries the entire section, including the lines that did not change — select the section in Hiration, delete it, paste. A partial paste means reading the existing block, working out where the new text goes, and not duplicating what is already there: three chances to get it wrong, silently, in a field that cannot easily be diffed afterwards.
