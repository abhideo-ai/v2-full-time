Role overview

Define and drive the architecture of next-generation scalable, reliable, high-performance backend platforms. Design scalable technical solutions, define architecture standards, drive modernization, provide technical leadership across engineering teams. Work with Product, Engineering, Frontend, Backend, DevOps to translate business requirements into scalable solutions.

Key responsibilities

Fourteen items, in the posting's own order.

  1. Design and oversee architecture for scalable, distributed, microservices-based systems
  2. Define and drive system architecture, technical standards, design principles
  3. Translate business and product requirements into scalable, reliable technical solutions
  4. Collaborate with Product, Backend, Frontend, DevOps and Engineering on architecture
  5. Design systems handling high traffic, high concurrency, large-scale workloads
  6. Drive improvements in performance, scalability, availability, reliability, latency
  7. Define and maintain standards for coding, Application Programming Interface (API) design, security, performance
  8. Lead architectural reviews, technical discussions, design evaluations
  9. Drive modernization and platform transformation: containerization, Continuous Integration / Continuous Delivery (CI/CD), cloud adoption, observability
  10. Evaluate emerging technologies on scalability, reliability, performance, cost efficiency
  11. Identify and address technical debt and architectural bottlenecks
  12. Provide technical mentorship to Senior Engineers, Tech Leads, development teams
  13. Work with DevOps and Platform teams on deployment, monitoring, scalability, operational efficiency
  14. Ensure architecture decisions align with long-term technology strategy

Required technical skills

Six groups, reproduced with the posting's own emphasis. Where the posting bolded a technology, it is bolded here. Where it wrote preferably, that word is kept — it is the difference between a hard requirement and a preference, and it is what a generous-but-honest read turns on.

Backend & Development

  • Strong hands-on experience with Java
  • Strong expertise in Spring Boot
  • Extensive experience with Microservices Architecture
  • Strong understanding of Representational State Transfer (REST) APIs and API design principles
  • Strong knowledge of Object-Oriented Programming and software engineering principles

System Design & Architecture

  • Strong expertise in System Design and Solution Architecture
  • Deep understanding of scalability patterns and distributed systems
  • Experience designing high-availability and high-performance systems
  • Strong understanding of caching, asynchronous processing, messaging, event-driven architecture
  • Experience with fault tolerance, load balancing, resiliency, performance optimization

Cloud & Infrastructure

  • Strong experience with Amazon Web Services (AWS) Cloud Platform
  • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes
  • Strong understanding of CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices
  • Experience with cloud-native architecture and modernization

Databases

  • Strong experience with Structured Query Language (SQL) databases, preferably MySQL
  • Experience with non-relational (NoSQL) databases, preferably Redis
  • Understanding of database performance, indexing, caching, optimization

Observability & Reliability

  • Grafana, Prometheus, Elasticsearch / Logstash / Kibana (ELK) Stack
  • Application monitoring, logging, metrics, tracing, system reliability

Leadership & Soft Skills

  • Technical leadership across engineering teams; stakeholder management
  • Influence technical decisions, drive architecture strategy
  • Mentor senior engineers and technical leads

Good to have

  • Large-scale, high-concurrency consumer platforms; systems serving millions of users
  • Kafka or other messaging platforms
  • Application security and compliance best practices
  • Leading technology migrations, platform modernization, large-scale redesign
  • Product-based or consumer internet companies
  • Exposure to Artificial Intelligence (AI) / Generative AI (GenAI)-enabled applications
