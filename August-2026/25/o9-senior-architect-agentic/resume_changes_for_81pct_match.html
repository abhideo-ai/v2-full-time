Ramp item, or capability cap

The distinction decides what a rewrite can and cannot do. A ramp item is a named tool he has not used, sitting on a foundation he demonstrably has — it is weeks of ramp, and it is not a capability he would be learning from zero. A capability cap is absent experience, and no wording closes it.
