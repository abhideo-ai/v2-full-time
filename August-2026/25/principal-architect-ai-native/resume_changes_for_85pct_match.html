Ramp item, or capability cap

Both terms mean something specific here. A ramp item is a named tool or vocabulary he has not used but whose underlying capability he demonstrably owns — the posting’s “e.g. / or / preferably” qualifiers are read generously, and a named product he has not run does not cap the capability. A capability cap is a genuinely core required technology that is absent, and honesty caps the score there regardless of how the résumé is worded.
