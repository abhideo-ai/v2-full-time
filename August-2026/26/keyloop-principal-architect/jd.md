## Role summary

Shape the technical direction of Keyloop's automotive SaaS platform. Define cross-cutting
architectural patterns and standards. Lead multidisciplinary architecture initiatives spanning
product, infrastructure and data. Research emerging technologies and make build / buy / adopt
recommendations. Own standards for multi-tenancy, data sovereignty and regulatory compliance.
Champion developer experience and engineering productivity as a platform-level outcome. Influence
strategy at senior leadership level. Coach Architects and Senior Architects; contribute to
return-on-investment (ROI) analysis for major technical investments.
