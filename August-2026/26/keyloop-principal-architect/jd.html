Role summary

Shape the technical direction of Keyloop’s automotive software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform. Define cross-cutting architectural patterns and standards. Lead multidisciplinary architecture initiatives spanning product, infrastructure and data. Research emerging technologies and make build / buy / adopt recommendations. Own architectural standards for multi-tenancy, data sovereignty, and regulatory compliance. Champion developer experience and engineering productivity as a platform-level outcome. Influence strategy at senior leadership level. Coach Architects and Senior Architects; contribute to return-on-investment (ROI) analysis for major technical investments.

What they’re looking for

Seven lines, in the posting’s own order. Where the posting hedged with e.g., that word is kept — it is the difference between a hard requirement and a preference, and it is what a generous-but-honest read turns on.

  1. 15+ years software development, 5+ in a senior architecture role
  2. Demonstrated track record of leading enterprise-scale architecture transformation
  3. Large-scale SaaS or multi-tenant platform architecture
  4. Mastery across cloud platforms, distributed systems, and platform engineering
  5. Deep understanding of observability, site reliability engineering (SRE), and cost optimisation
  6. Strong data architecture — scalable, reliable, analytically capable systems
  7. Executive-level communication; influence without authority; balance long-term vision with near-term delivery
