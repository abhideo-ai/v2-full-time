Why it is worded this way

  • Three questions, three answers, in his order. He asked take a look, are you interested, send the CV. The reply confirms both documents were read, states the interest and which of the two roles, and says the CV is attached. Then the call. Nothing is added and nothing is asked back.
  • Availability leads. Available immediately, nothing to serve. The documents put an expected start of 5 October 2026 on the seat, and Andela is staffing a contractor engagement against that date, so a candidate who can start without a notice period is the single most useful fact in the message. It comes before any technical detail.
  • Principal is named, and Lead is not declined. He was approached for the Principal seat. Saying which one he wants to take forward answers the "if this doesn't quite fit" line without volunteering a downgrade in writing. If Paul wants to route him to the Lead bar, that is Paul's move to make on the call, and the door stays open because the reply never shuts it.
  • The strongest line goes first among the technical points. "Daily use of AI coding assistants such as GitHub Copilot, Cursor or Claude Code, with concrete examples of productivity gains, guardrails and rollout to other engineers" is a required bullet on both documents, it is one of the six named "Your Skills" traits, and it is a standing responsibility — three appearances in one document. The rollout half of that sentence appears only on the Principal document. His measured Claude Code and OpenAI Codex rollout across a 20-engineer team answers it exactly, and very few candidates can evidence it. It gets its own paragraph rather than being buried in a list.
  • The stack paragraph is a match list, not a boast. Every item in it is named by the documents themselves: FastAPI, retrieval-augmented generation, LangChain and LangGraph, Model Context Protocol tool integration, OpenSearch as a vector store, AWS with infrastructure-as-code, Kubernetes, OAuth2/OIDC, and regulated business-to-business information services with healthcare named explicitly. It reads as recognition of their description rather than a recital of his.
  • Evening hours are offered without being discussed. The documents state a firm 6-hour daily overlap with Central Standard Time, roughly 7:30 PM to 1:30 AM India time. Offering an evening slot for the call signals comfort with that window as a fact rather than raising it as a topic. The substantive conversation about it happens out loud.
  • The call is concrete. Friday 28 August, two days out rather than tomorrow, a window rather than a single minute, and the phone number in the same line.
  • Every claim in the message is on the résumé and safe to claim. No Amura outcome metric appears, because none exists. No number appears that is not already carried by the master résumé.

What was deliberately left out

Outward-facing messages lead with strengths and do not advertise gaps. All three items below are real, all three will surface, and all three are handled far better out loud than pre-emptively conceded in a written reply that Paul may forward verbatim to the client.

  • The Python-years requirement. The Principal document asks for 8+ years of hands-on production Python (the Lead document asks 5+). His production Python is the Amura programme, roughly fourteen months, March 2025 to April 2026. This is a duration cap and no wording closes it. It is not mentioned, not hinted at, and not softened anywhere in the message — and equally, nothing in the message implies more Python years than he has. The message never states a Python duration at all. Expect it to come up on the call, and answer it plainly when it does. The honest frame is 19+ years of professional engineering with recent production Python on an AI platform, not a longer Python history.
  • Software development kit (SDK) design, versioning and distribution. Required at 4+ years on the Principal document and 3+ on the Lead, with its own responsibility bullet on both — packaging, semantic versioning, Python Package Index (PyPI) or an internal registry, and managing breaking changes across consumers. This is unevidenced anywhere in his record and is not manufactured here. It is left out of the message entirely rather than being approximated with adjacent work.
  • Azure. Both documents say "Azure strongly preferred" and name Azure Functions, App Service, Azure Kubernetes Service, Azure OpenAI Service, Azure DevOps, Azure Monitor, Azure AI Search, Bicep and ARM. He has not used Azure and it is never claimed. Both documents also accept AWS, which is his, so the message claims AWS and says nothing about Azure in either direction. Volunteering the gap in writing invites a filter before a conversation; the preference is real and belongs on the call.
  • Compensation. He was not asked, so it is not volunteered. Not the expected band, not the current figure, not a rate. See the line below for the call.
  • Every logistics question. Employer of record for the first 12 months, what conversion is actually conditional on, whether the overlap window persists after conversion, device-management requirements, and what the skill test covers are all genuinely open and all genuinely worth knowing. None of them is in the reply. Each one is another round trip standing between him and a phone call, and every one of them gets a better answer spoken than written.

Raise these on the call, out loud

These are the real-world gates on this seat. They sit outside both scores and none of them is a reason not to have the call. They are reasons to have it before anything is signed.

  • Contract-to-hire, and what conversion actually depends on. Twelve months as a full-time contractor, then "intended" conversion to permanent Wolters Kluwer India employment, "subject to performance and business need". Both documents use that qualifier. Worth asking directly: who is the employer of record for the first year, Andela or Wolters Kluwer; what performance bar and whose headcount and budget the conversion depends on; what happens at month twelve if it does not convert; and how benefits, leave and continuity of service carry across the boundary.
  • The 7:30 PM to 1:30 AM India-time window, stated as firm. Six hours of daily overlap with Central Standard Time, and both documents call it "a firm requirement". Ask whether it holds for the full twelve months, whether it persists after conversion and relocation to a hybrid office, and whether the six hours are fixed hours or a six-hour overlap he can position within the day.
  • Bring your own device. No hardware is supplied. Ask what security, encryption or mobile-device-management requirements come attached, and whether the client will require management software installed on a machine he owns.
  • Relocation to Pune or Chennai on conversion, hybrid three days per week. Relocation is not a constraint for him, but the timing, the trigger and who bears the cost are all unstated in the documents.
  • The GenAI Engineering Skill Test. It sits ahead of the technical and behavioural interview on both documents, and neither says what it is. Ask what it covers, what format it takes, how long it runs and whether it is timed or take-home. Recorded here as a fact about the process. No practice material is being built for it — preparation waits until a company responds and a process actually reaches that stage.
  • Which bar he is being put forward for. Two documents arrived. Worth hearing Paul say which one goes to the client, and whether the client is filling one seat or two.
