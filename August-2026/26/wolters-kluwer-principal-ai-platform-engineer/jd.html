Role overviews, as written

Principal AI Platform Engineer

"Wolters Kluwer is seeking a hands-on Principal AI Platform Engineer for the Platform Engineering Team. This is the most senior individual-contributor engineering role on the team. You will set the technical direction for the AI platform, design production-grade AI and software services and SDKs that other teams build on, and raise the delivery bar through rigorous AI-assisted engineering."

"The ideal candidate is an elite Python engineer with a track record of owning platform-level architecture at scale, deep expertise in Web APIs, SDK design and cloud platforms, and production-level mastery of LLMs, RAG and prompt engineering. Closing line: "Build the AI platform other engineering teams build on."

Lead AI Platform Engineer

"Wolters Kluwer is seeking a hands-on Lead AI Platform Engineer for the Platform Engineering Team. This role designs and builds production-grade AI and software services and SDKs, accelerates delivery using AI-assisted development, and partners closely with product and platform teams to ship reliable software quickly."

"The ideal candidate is an expert Python engineer who pairs effectively with AI tools, has deep experience with Web APIs, SDK design and cloud platforms, and brings practical production-level expertise with LLMs, RAG and prompt engineering. You provide technical direction for a feature area or service, review others' work and raise the engineering bar without requiring formal people-management responsibility."

Closing line: "Build reliable AI platform capabilities that engineering teams can ship on."

Both bars are individual contributor on their face. The Principal document says explicitly that leadership is through "architecture, standards and influence, not headcount"; the Lead document says the role raises the engineering bar "without requiring formal people-management responsibility."

The employment shape, and what to ask on the call

This is contract-to-hire, not permanent from day one. The PDFs confirm what the InMail implied and add the conditionality: twelve months as a full-time contractor, then conversion to permanent Wolters Kluwer India employment "subject to performance and business need". Andela is the intermediary for that first year — an agency, not in-house. That is distinct from the ModMed route, where the recruiter is ModMed's own talent-acquisition lead.

Open, and to be asked out loud on the call — not in writing, and not as added questions in a reply:

  • Who is the employer of record for the first 12 months — Andela or Wolters Kluwer?
  • What is conversion conditional on in practice? Performance against what bar, whose headcount, whose budget?
  • What happens if it does not convert at 12 months?
  • Benefits, leave and continuity of service across the conversion boundary.
  • Which bar is he actually being put forward for — Principal or Lead? Both documents were sent.
  • Is the 7:30 PM – 1:30 AM IST window fixed for the full 12 months, and does it persist after conversion and relocation?
  • Does "bring your own device" carry any security, encryption or device-management requirement?
  • What does the GenAI Engineering Skill Test cover, and what is its format and duration?

The binding constraint — Python duration

His production Python is the Amura programme, roughly fourteen months (March 2025 – April 2026). The documents ask for 8+ years on the Principal bar and 5+ years on the Lead bar of hands-on production Python. This is a duration cap. No rewrite closes it, and no Python years may be invented. It is the one requirement where the Lead bar is materially more reachable than the Principal bar — and it is still not met.

The second real gap — SDK design, versioning and PyPI distribution

Unevidenced anywhere in his record. Required at 4+ years on the Principal bar and 3+ years on the Lead bar, and it carries a dedicated responsibility bullet on both documents on top of the experience line. Not to be manufactured.
