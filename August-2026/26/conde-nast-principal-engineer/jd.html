v1 findings that stand

Everything v1 built is copied into v1/ in this workspace — both research pages, the 96-point change analysis, the unsent reply, and the 18 August PDF (Portable Document Format export). v1’s research established the following, and it stands:

  • The JD is verbatim the live Solution Architect requisition — same bullets, same order, same duplicated-word typo “working on on Agentic AI projects”. Today’s LinkedIn body carries that identical typo. Principal Engineer is the internal title for the Solution Architect requisition.
  • The recruiter is verified real — corroborated across four business-contact databases; the email conforms to the documented first_last@condenast.com pattern.
  • “3+ years as a Tech Lead, Staff Engineer, OR Architect” is the public wording; the recruiter compressed it to “3 years architect”. The public version is the more generous one and is the one on this posting.
  • Python AND Node.js both named and both production-confirmed — Django, FastAPI, LangChain, LangGraph; Node.js/Express at Deque and the pre-Go chat service.

  • Advertising / Subscriptions / Commerce is a PLUS, not a requirement. He has real commerce — Teletext travel, the +65% hotel conversion lift, 25 suppliers over a MuleSoft enterprise service bus (ESB).
  • The binding technical constraint is the production SCOPE of the agentic work, not its existence — CLAUDE.md records the split: KQ1’s claim grammar is live, KQ9’s narrator ships off by default. Surface it; never rewrite it.
