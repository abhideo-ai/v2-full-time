Condé Nast Technology Lab · Solution Architect diligence (Bengaluru)

Who is hiring: Condé Nast — the global media/publishing house behind Vogue, GQ, Wired, The New Yorker, Vanity Fair, Architectural Digest (AD), Bon Appétit, Allure, Condé Nast Traveler and more — posting through its India engineering arm, the Condé Nast Technology Lab (Bengaluru + Chennai). A named, decades-old public-facing global brand; existence is not in question. Plain-English glossary (concepts, not just expansions):

Solution Architect (as a level)
A senior individual-contributor / Staff-architect seat: you own the end-to-end technical design of a domain and influence many teams, but typically through architecture and mentorship, not a chain of direct reports. The scope here is genuinely senior (partnering with Engineering VPs/Directors), but the title and grade usually sit in an IC ladder below a VP of Engineering — the leveling nuance for an 18-year VP.
AI-native / AI-assisted development
Building software with AI code-generation tools (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot) as a first-class, governed practice — how the tooling plugs into continuous integration / continuous delivery (CI/CD), code review, test automation and production-readiness gates. This is the JD's headline theme and maps directly onto the candidate's measured Claude-Code / Codex rollout.
Agentic AI (in production)
Software where a Large Language Model (LLM) plans and calls tools autonomously — agent orchestration, tool use, evaluation frameworks, guardrail design, multi-agent pipelines. The JD asks for hands-on production experience here; the candidate's LangChain / LangGraph + Model Context Protocol (MCP) + guardrail work is the closest evidence he brings.
Captive engineering lab / Global Capability Centre (GCC)
A company-owned engineering unit in India that builds for the global parent (here, the platform behind the media brands) rather than for external clients. Comp is benchmarked to the parent's internal India bands — for a media company these tend to trail product-company / FAANG bands.
"Responses managed off LinkedIn"
The apply button does not collect the application inside LinkedIn — it routes to Condé Nast's own careers portal / applicant-tracking system (ATS), where he creates a candidate profile. Not a LinkedIn Easy Apply.

The leveling question senior scope, but an IC/architect title vs his VP

Title vs his current levelHe is an 18+ year VP of Technology / former Co-Founder. "Solution Architect" is nominally an IC / Staff-architect title — a leveling consideration on paper, even though the JD's scope reads Staff/Principal-Architect (owns a domain end-to-end, partners with Engineering VPs and Directors, coaches engineers across levels). The seat's influence is senior; the title/grade may sit below a VP-of-Engineering line.
Reports vs influenceArchitect seats usually lead through architecture and mentorship, not a direct-report org. The JD's "coach and mentor engineers across levels … elevating capability beyond the immediate team" reads as influence-based leadership, likely with few or no direct reports. For a candidate who built and ran a 0→20+ org, that's a real shape change to go in eyes-open about — not necessarily bad (architect tracks can pay and carry weight), but different from a people-leadership VP seat.
What to confirmGrade/band (is this Principal/Staff-scope or a mid-architect rung?), fixed-cash range, direct reports vs pure technical influence, and which domain/ecosystem the seat owns. These four decide whether this is a lateral (senior architect, strong comp) or a step down (mid-IC title, capped band).

This is a real, senior, cross-domain architecture seat at a marquee global media company, and the JD is unusually well-matched to his AI-assisted-development-governance and agentic story — few architect candidates bring a measured Claude-Code/Codex rollout plus LangGraph/MCP/guardrail work, and Condé Nast is genuinely investing in AI (OpenAI/Amazon licensing, ~70 pilots, Databricks/AWS/Bedrock). Forcing questions (get answered early)

  1. Fixed-cash band + grade — what's the fixed-cash range, and what internal grade is "Solution Architect" (Principal/Staff-scope, or a mid-architect rung)? Does it reach ₹75L+? (Floor is ₹75L–₹1Cr cash; resolve before deep cycles.)
  2. Reports vs influence — does this seat carry direct reports, or is it pure technical influence + mentorship across teams?
  3. Which domain / ecosystem — which part of the global platform does the seat own, and what's the current architecture maturity (greenfield vs. sustaining a large existing estate)?
  4. Agentic-AI-in-production reality — how mature is the org's agentic AI in production today (real multi-agent pipelines, evals, guardrails), vs. pilots? (Tells him how much of the JD is aspirational.)
  5. AWS vs Azure — is the platform AWS-first (his depth) or genuinely multi-cloud with Azure? (Scopes the ramp.)
  6. Work mode — hybrid / remote / onsite in Bengaluru (Lavelle Road)? Cadence?
