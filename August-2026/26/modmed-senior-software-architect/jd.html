Flags recorded at intake — carried, not resolved

Not part of the posting. These are the three honesty flags jd.md recorded when the seat arrived, reproduced here so the required list above is never read without them. Each is honoured throughout the workspace and none is silently resolved.

1. “Event Streaming (e.g. Kafka, NATS)” is an e.g. qualifier, and Kafka is on the never-claim list. Read generously per the scoring rule: his Amazon Kinesis two-stream Enhanced Fan-Out design, confirmed in production, satisfies “event streaming”, and the criterion is scored as met. Kafka and NATS are never written as his — not in the rubric, not on the résumé, not in any artefact here.

2. “Reactive / Functional programming”. Spring WebFlux is on the never-claim list and is not claimed. The posting asks only for familiarity, so this is a ramp item, not a capability cap. Asynchronous and event-driven work is cited as what it is and is not offered as reactive programming in the Project Reactor sense.

3. “Distributed Cache Grids”. Redis is claimable (user-confirmed 2026-08-25) and sits in the production request path of systems he owned. A named grid product — Hazelcast, Ignite, Coherence — is not his and is claimed nowhere. The posting names no product, so the requirement is the discipline, not the brand.
