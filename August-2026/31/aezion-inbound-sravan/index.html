Glossary — every acronym and unfamiliar term on this page, expanded (15)
  • TAG — talent acquisition group; the in-house recruiting function. A genuine collision worth naming: TAG is also Google Tag Manager, a marketing tool. At Aezion the expansion is settled by the company’s own published applications inbox, tag@aezion.com.
  • URL — uniform resource locator; the web address of a posting. There is none for this seat, and source_url on the tracker row is NULL rather than prose, because the launcher renders that column as a link
  • JD — job description; the abbreviation behind the jd.md and jd.html filenames, and behind “Full-time JD workspace” in the breadcrumb above
  • AI — artificial intelligence
  • SaaS — software as a service. Multi-tenant SaaS is one running system serving many customer organisations with their data isolated from each other
  • IC — individual contributor; an engineering track with no people-management mandate
  • ATS — applicant tracking system; the software that holds requisitions and applications. Aezion runs none, which is why no Aezion posting can be dated and no requisition can be cross-referenced
  • CIN — corporate identification number; the unique identifier India’s Ministry of Corporate Affairs assigns to a registered company
  • GCC — global capability centre; an offshore centre operated for a single named client, often with a path to the client eventually owning it. Not the Gulf Cooperation Council.
  • BOT — build-operate-transfer; the vendor stands up a team in India, runs it for a period, then hands it to the client. The consequence for an individual is that the employer can change mid-engagement, by design
  • Employer of record — the legal employer, which under a dedicated-team or GCC arrangement is the vendor rather than the company whose work is actually being done
  • RAG — retrieval-augmented generation; answering with a language model over documents fetched at query time rather than from the model’s own weights
  • HIPAA — Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, the United States health-data protection law
  • HTML — HyperText Markup Language · PDF — portable document format
  • Naukri — a large Indian job board, and the surface carrying two Aezion listings that do not appear on Aezion’s own careers page
